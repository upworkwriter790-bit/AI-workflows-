# AI Operating System — selling script

For voiceover/captions over **Ilactic's own AIOS demo footage** (not the reference clips —
see the note in chat about why). Built pain-first, each beat sized for a social cut
(15–45s) or strung together for a longer explainer. Bracketed notes are the visual to
cut to; swap them for your real screens once there's something to film.

---

### Hook (0–5s) — the pain, cold open
> "Your business doesn't run in one place. It runs in six tabs, four group chats,
> and whatever your best employee remembers."

[visual: quick-cut montage — inbox, WhatsApp, spreadsheet, a sticky note]

### Agitate (5–15s) — name the specific cost
> "A lead goes cold because nobody followed up. An invoice sits unpaid because nobody
> noticed. Stock runs out because nobody was watching. Not because your team is bad —
> because no single person can watch everything, all the time."

[visual: a stat ticking up — "3 invoices overdue", "2 leads untouched", "stock low"]

### Turn (15–20s) — introduce the product
> "This is the AI Operating System. One place that actually watches your business —
> and a dedicated AI teammate for every part of it."

[visual: dashboard opening, department sidebar appearing one by one]

### Mechanism, beat 1 — built for YOUR business (20–28s)
> "Tell it what you do. It sets itself up around your business — not a generic
> template with your logo slapped on it."

[visual: industry/business-type picker → workspace reconfiguring live]

### Mechanism, beat 2 — a real teammate per department (28–36s)
> "Every department — sales, finance, marketing, ops — gets its own AI agent.
> Not a chatbot bolted on the side. One that actually knows that department's work."

[visual: department-by-department walkthrough, one agent avatar per screen]

### Mechanism, beat 3 — the trust moment (36–45s) — THE key beat, don't cut this
> "And here's the part that matters most: it never acts on its own. It drafts.
> It flags. It recommends. You approve — or you don't. Nothing moves without your yes."

[visual: a drafted action card on screen, cursor clicking Approve, confirmation toast]

### Proof (45–52s)
> "Ask it one question and watch it pull in the right specialists, show you exactly
> why, and bring back one answer — not five dashboards to cross-check yourself."

[visual: one query typed in → multiple department cards lighting up → combined answer]

### Close / CTA (52–60s)
> "Stop running your business from memory. Let's build the version that runs itself —
> with you still holding every approval."
> **"Talk to us. [ilactic.com/aios → "Talk to us"]"**

## Lines to reuse as on-screen captions (short, punchy, match the beat they sit over)
- "Built around your business, not a template."
- "A real teammate per department."
- "Nothing moves without your yes."
- "One question. The right specialists. One answer."
- "Stop running your business from memory."

## What NOT to say (keeps claims honest + avoids overpromising)
- Don't claim it "runs your business for you" — the whole sell is that a human still
  approves everything. Say "runs itself, with you approving" not "runs itself."
- Don't invent specific named AI personas (no "meet Finn, meet Nova") unless you
  actually decide to build Ilactic-branded agent personas later — that's a real
  decision to make deliberately, not borrow from a reference video.
- Don't show a specific client's real data in any screen recording without their
  separate sign-off — use seeded/demo data only, same as the reference videos did.
